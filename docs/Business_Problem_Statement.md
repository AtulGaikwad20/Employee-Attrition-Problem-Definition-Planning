# Employee Attrition Problem Statement

## 1. Introduction

Employee attrition occurs when employees leave an organization. It is an important business problem because employees play a major role in the success of a company. High attrition can increase recruitment and training costs, reduce productivity, and affect team performance.

This project uses HR data to understand employee attrition and identify patterns that may be associated with employees leaving the organization.

## 2. Business Problem

Organizations may find it difficult to identify employees who are more likely to leave. If the organization cannot understand the factors related to attrition, it may face increased hiring costs, loss of experienced employees, and interruptions in daily operations.

The objective of this project is to analyze employee data and develop a machine learning approach to predict whether an employee may leave the organization.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Understand employee attrition using HR data.
- Identify employee characteristics associated with attrition.
- Explore patterns in the dataset using exploratory data analysis.
- Develop and evaluate a machine learning model to predict attrition.
- Support HR teams in planning fair and effective employee retention strategies.

## 4. Business Impact of Attrition

High employee attrition can result in:

- Increased recruitment and onboarding costs.
- Loss of experienced and skilled employees.
- Reduced productivity during employee replacement.
- Additional workload for remaining employees.
- Loss of organizational knowledge.
- Lower team stability and continuity.

## 5. Business Stakeholders

The main stakeholders are:

- **HR Managers:** Understand attrition patterns and plan retention activities.
- **Employees:** Benefit from fair workplace policies and improved working conditions.
- **Department Managers:** Identify team-level concerns and support employees.
- **Senior Management:** Make informed decisions about workforce planning.
- **Data Analysts and Data Scientists:** Analyze data and evaluate predictive models.

## 6. Business Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs can help evaluate attrition and retention:

- **Attrition Rate:** Percentage of employees who leave during a defined period.
- **Employee Retention Rate:** Percentage of employees retained during a defined period.
- **Recruitment Cost:** Average cost of hiring a replacement employee.
- **Employee Turnover Cost:** Estimated cost associated with employees leaving.
- **Model Recall:** Percentage of employees who actually leave that the model correctly identifies.
- **Model ROC-AUC:** Measures how well the model distinguishes between employees who leave and those who stay.

Business KPIs should be calculated from suitable organizational data. Model metrics measure prediction performance and are not direct measures of business improvement.

## 7. Employee Retention Strategies

Organizations may consider:

- Reviewing employee workload and work-life balance.
- Providing career development and training opportunities.
- Collecting employee feedback through appropriate surveys.
- Reviewing compensation and recognition policies.
- Supporting managers in improving team communication.
- Investigating workplace concerns fairly and confidentially.

These strategies should be based on employee feedback and reliable evidence rather than predictions alone.

## 8. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is an HR analytics solution that explains attrition patterns and provides predictions that can support workforce planning. The system is intended to assist HR professionals, not replace human judgment or make automatic employment decisions.

## 9. Conclusion

Employee attrition is an important organizational challenge. By studying HR data and using appropriate analytical methods, this project aims to help organizations better understand attrition patterns and explore responsible ways to improve employee retention.
